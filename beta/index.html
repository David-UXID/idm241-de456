Original interface: septa.org/schedules

1. Triggers

Interaction 1: Route rows

Original

  • Hover
  • Hover off
  • Click on the chevron button beside a route name (only on some routes)

Improved

  • Hover
  • Hover off
  • Click or keyboard activation anywhere on the route row

Interaction 2: Find a Route / Station / Trip tabs and search field

Original

  • Hover
  • Hover off
  • Click on a tab
  • Click on the search field

Improved

  • Hover
  • Hover off
  • Click or keyboard activation (Tab, Enter, arrow keys, Esc) on a tab or the search field
  • Typing in the search field

2. Rules

What rules determine how each microinteraction behaves?

Interaction 1: Route rows

Original

On hover: the pointer becomes a finger only over the colored route name or the separate chevron button.

Off hover: the pointer returns to normal.

On click: only the chevron button opens the schedule. Some routes have no chevron, so it is unclear whether they can be opened.

Improved

On hover: the pointer becomes a finger and the whole route row lightens.

Off hover: the pointer returns to normal and the row returns to its original color.

On click or keyboard activation: the entire row is one target. It toggles the schedule open or closed, and a chevron appears on every expandable route.

Interaction 2: Find a Route / Station / Trip tabs and search field

Original

On hover: the pointer becomes a finger over a tab or the search field.

Off hover: the pointer returns to normal.

On click: a tab turns black with white text and shows a small pointer beneath it. The search field shows both a magnifying glass and a dropdown chevron, so it is unclear whether it is a text input or a list.

Improved

On hover: the pointer becomes a finger and the tab or search field lightens. Unselected tabs have a visible border.

Off hover: the pointer returns to normal and the tab or field returns to its original color.

On click or keyboard activation: a tab becomes selected with a checkmark and underline in addition to the black fill, and the panel below changes. The whole search field is one target that opens a suggestions list, typing filters it, and choosing a result fills the field and closes the list.

Beta description

Improved versions of the microinteractions on the SEPTA Schedules page.

The current Schedules page works, but it does not always show riders what is clickable, which choice is active, or that a click registered. This beta improves three things:

1. Clearer selected state

The active transit mode is obvious at a glance.

2. One-target route rows

The whole route row opens the schedule, with a chevron showing open or closed.

3. Immediate click feedback

Chevron rotation, smooth reveal, and a brief highlight confirm each click.

1. Make the selection state clearer

Metro, Bus, Regional Rail, and SEPTA Access look alike. Only a dark fill marks the selected one.

Improvement: the selected button gets a checkmark and an accent underline in addition to the dark fill, so it does not rely on color alone. State is exposed with aria-pressed.

2. Improve the route expansion interaction

Some routes have a separate colored arrow beside the route name, which splits the interaction into two targets.

Improvement: the entire route row is one button. A chevron shows whether it is expanded or collapsed.

When a rider activates a route row, the schedule opens beneath that row. A chevron points down while collapsed and rotates to indicate the expanded state.

3. Give immediate feedback after a click

Riders should know right away that a route opened or a category changed.

Interaction feedback: the chevron rotates, the schedule slides open, and the row or panel flashes a brief highlight and border after activation.

Motion: animations are turned off for riders who prefer reduced motion. The open state still shows through the chevron direction and aria-expanded.
