2. Rules

What rules determine how each microinteraction behaves?

Interaction 1: Route rows

Improved

On hover: the whole route row is highlighted.

On click: the schedule opens beneath the row and its chevron turns up. Clicking the same row again hides the schedule and turns the chevron down.

Interaction 2: Find a Route / Find a Station / Plan your trip

Improved

On click: the selected tab gets a dark fill, checkmark, and underline, and its matching panel appears.

On the trip search field: clicking it opens suggestions. Typing filters the list; clicking a suggestion fills the field and closes the list.

4. Loops & modes

Interaction 1: Route rows

Improved

Clicking a route row toggles it between collapsed and expanded; clicking it again reverses the state. The video repeats this on different route rows.

Interaction 2: Find a Route / Find a Station / Plan your trip

Improved

The three tabs are modes: selecting one marks it active and replaces the panel with that category. In Plan your trip, the search loop is open suggestions, filter by typing, then choose a result to fill the field and close suggestions; this sequence can be repeated with another search.
