Beta description

Improved versions of the microinteractions on the SEPTA Schedules page.

The current Schedules page works, but it does not always show riders what is clickable, which choice is active, or that a click registered. This beta improves three things:

1. Clearer selected state

The active transit mode is obvious at a glance.

2. One-target route rows

The whole route row opens the schedule, with a chevron showing open or closed.

3. Immediate click feedback

Chevron rotation, smooth reveal, and a brief highlight confirm each click.

1. Make the selection state clearer

Metro, Bus, Regional Rail, and SEPTA Access look alike. Only a dark fill marks the selected one.

Improvement: the selected button gets a checkmark and an accent underline in addition to the dark fill, so it does not rely on color alone. State is exposed with aria-pressed.

2. Improve the route expansion interaction

Some routes have a separate colored arrow beside the route name, which splits the interaction into two targets.

Improvement: the entire route row is one button. A chevron shows whether it is expanded or collapsed.

3. Give immediate feedback after a click

Riders should know right away that a route opened or a category changed.

Improvement: try the demos above. The chevron rotates, the schedule slides open, and the row or panel flashes a brief highlight and border.

Motion: animations are turned off for riders who prefer reduced motion. The open state still shows through the chevron direction and aria-expanded.

Summary: triggers, rules, feedback

Triggers

  • Hover
  • Hover off
  • Click or keyboard activation

Rules

  • Hover: pointer becomes a finger and the row or button lightens.
  • Click on a mode: it becomes selected, others deselect.
  • Click on a route row: it toggles open or closed.

Feedback

  • Checkmark and underline on the selected mode.
  • Chevron rotates 180 degrees.
  • Schedule slides open and a brief highlight plays.

Loops & modes

None.
