          2. Rules

          What rules determine how each microinteraction behaves?

          Interaction 1: Route rows

          Improved

          On hover: the whole route row is highlighted.

          On click: the schedule opens beneath the row and its chevron turns up. Clicking the same row again hides the schedule and turns the chevron down.

          Interaction 2: Find a Route / Find a Station / Plan your trip

          Improved

          On click: the selected tab gets a dark fill, checkmark, and underline, and its matching panel appears.

          On the trip search field: clicking it opens suggestions. Typing filters the list; clicking a suggestion fills the field and closes the list.

          4. Loops & modes

          Interaction 1: Route rows

          Improved

          Clicking a route row toggles it between collapsed and expanded; clicking it again reverses the state. The video repeats this on different route rows.

          Interaction 2: Find a Route / Find a Station / Plan your trip

          Improved

          The three tabs are modes: selecting one marks it active and replaces the panel with that category. In Plan your trip, the search loop is open suggestions, filter by typing, then choose a result to fill the field and close suggestions; this sequence can be repeated with another search.

          1. Make the selection state clearer

          Metro, Bus, Regional Rail, and SEPTA Access look alike. Only a dark fill marks the selected one.

          Improvement: the selected button gets a checkmark and an accent underline in addition to the dark fill, so it does not rely on color alone. State is exposed with aria-pressed.

          2. Improve the route expansion interaction

          Some routes have a separate colored arrow beside the route name, which splits the interaction into two targets.

          Improvement: the entire route row is one button. A chevron shows whether it is expanded or collapsed.

          When a rider activates a route row, the schedule opens beneath that row. A chevron points down while collapsed and rotates to indicate the expanded state.

          3. Give immediate feedback after a click

          Riders should know right away that a route opened or a category changed.

          Interaction feedback: the chevron rotates, the schedule slides open, and the row or panel flashes a brief highlight and border after activation.

          Motion: animations are turned off for riders who prefer reduced motion. The open state still shows through the chevron direction and aria-expanded.
