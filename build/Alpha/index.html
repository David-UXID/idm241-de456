    Locating the interaction

    Where does a rider find the Schedules buttons on septa.org?

    The interaction is the row of Schedules mode buttons. Riders click through them to pick the type of service they want a schedule for.

    1. Go to septa.org, or open the Schedules page directly at septa.org/schedules.
    2. From the home page, choose Schedules in the site navigation. On a phone, open the Toggle navigation menu button first.
    3. On the Schedules page, find the page heading Schedules and the breadcrumb (Home > Schedules).
    4. Directly below the heading is a row of four buttons, one for each mode of transportation SEPTA publishes schedules for.

    Metro

    Subway, Elevated, and Trolleys. Selected by default.

    Bus

    Bus route schedules.

    Regional Rail

    Regional Rail line schedules.

    SEPTA Access

    Formerly CCT.

    Result: Selecting a button changes the panel below it to that mode's schedules, for example Metro Schedules with its list of lines.

    2. Rules

    What rules determine how the microinteraction behaves?

    Rule 1: On hover

    The pointer becomes a finger.

    Rule 2: Off hover

    The pointer returns to normal. The button and selected mode stay the same.

    Rule 3: On click

    When clicked, the background color changes to black and the text and icon change to white. After interaction, it displays schedule information underneath.

    Why it matters: The selected mode and schedule content stay clear and consistent.
